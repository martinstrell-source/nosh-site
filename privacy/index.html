Nosh Next

Privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Nosh Next helps you keep track of restaurants you want to try and suggests new ones that match your taste. This page says what the app keeps about you, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your email address, or the address Apple or Google gives the app when you sign in with them.
  • Your places: the restaurants you save, hide or mark as favorites, your notes, the visits you log, and where each place came from (a link, a screenshot, a friend).
  • Photos you add: photos of bills, food or storefronts, and screenshots you share to the app.
  • Lists you import, such as a Google Maps or Beli export.
  • Your taste profile, worked out from the places you save and how you rate them.
  • Your location, while you use the app, to show places near you. The app keeps a rough home area for suggestions. It doesn't track you in the background.
  • Which days you open the app, to see whether people keep using it.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or freezes, a report with the phone model, system version and what went wrong. It doesn't include your name or email.

Who else sees it

Nosh Next doesn't sell your information or show ads. It uses these services to run:

  • Supabase stores your account and data.
  • Google Maps Platform looks up places, ratings and hours, using the place or area you're searching.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads what you share, such as a link, a screenshot or a photo, to work out which restaurant it means, and helps pick suggestions. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes by email.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.

Deleting your data

In the app, scroll to the bottom of the list, tap your email to open Account, and tap Delete account. That deletes your account and everything in it right away, photos included. If you signed in with Apple, the app also gives up its access to your Apple ID. You can also ask by email; see Deleting your account.

Children

Nosh Next isn't meant for children under 13.
