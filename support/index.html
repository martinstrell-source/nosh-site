Common questions

I signed in and my places are gone. You may have signed in a different way than before. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, that makes a separate account. Sign out at the bottom of the list and sign in with the email you used before.

How do I add a place? Share a link, a screenshot or a photo to Nosh Next, or tap Add a place and type a name.

How do I delete my account? Scroll to the bottom of the list and tap Delete account. See Privacy for what that removes.
